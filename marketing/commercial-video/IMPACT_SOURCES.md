# TrueGuard impact commercial — source ledger

The high-energy commercial uses original footage plates and code-native graphics. No footage from the four editing references is included in the final export.

## Verified claims shown in the cut

### Investor confusion and the ZOOM suspension

- Primary source: [U.S. SEC Order of Suspension of Trading, Zoom Technologies, Inc., Release No. 34-88477](https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/suspensions/2020/34-88477-o.pdf)
- Date: March 25, 2020.
- Claim used: the SEC cited concerns about investors confusing Zoom Technologies with a similarly named Nasdaq-listed communications company.
- Visual: a screenshot of page one of the official SEC order, with the relevant paragraph highlighted.

### Execution price and available liquidity

- Primary source: [Investor.gov — Understanding Order Types](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-14)
- Claim used: the price of a market order is not guaranteed and can differ because of available liquidity at execution.
- The small-holder order-book sequence is explicitly marked as an illustrative visualization. It is not represented as a real trade.

### Liquidity in tokenised markets

- Primary source: [Monetary Authority of Singapore speech — Tokenisation in financial services: pathways to scale](https://www.bis.org/review/r241104k.htm)
- Date: November 4, 2024.
- Claim used: tokenisation efforts have created fragmentation and deeper liquidity is needed across primary and secondary markets.

## Product accuracy

- The SPCXUSD X-Perp screen is a TrueGuard research example and is not described as SpaceX equity.
- ExitTogether combines only compatible orders, seeks a large-order quote and preserves each holder's minimum.
- ExitTogether does not guarantee a better price, a quote or execution.
- The final card retains `Research is not investment advice.`
