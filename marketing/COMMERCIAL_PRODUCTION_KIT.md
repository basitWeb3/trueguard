# TrueGuard commercial production kit

## Campaign idea

**The story. The product. The exit.**

The commercial should make three ideas clear without explaining blockchain jargon:

1. Understand what the real asset actually does.
2. Check what the token really gives its holder.
3. Let matching small sellers seek one large-order quote together.

## Final voiceover — 30 seconds

> RWA buyers often know the story. But not always what the token gives them. TrueGuard connects the real asset to the onchain product, checks the user's intent, and links every important claim to proof. When matching small holders want to sell, ExitTogether pools their orders to seek one large-order quote—without removing each holder's minimum. TrueGuard. Understand it. Verify it. Exit together.

## CapCut visual settings

- Master versions: `3840 × 2160` landscape and `2160 × 3840` vertical.
- Timeline: 24 fps.
- Headline font: Space Grotesk Bold.
- Supporting font: Manrope Medium.
- Technical labels: DM Mono Medium.
- Background: `#F4F6F1`.
- Carbon: `#111713`.
- Emerald: `#137A51`.
- Bright signal: `#67E7A0`.
- Mismatch: `#C53F49`.
- Use straight cuts, restrained 4–6 frame dissolves and one glass-light wipe. Avoid template-heavy transitions.
- Keep captions inside the center 80% of the vertical canvas so X and Reels controls do not cover them.

## Higgsfield direction

Use the same stills as anchor images in Cinema Studio. Start conservative:

- Product-film look, 50 mm lens for the wide scenes.
- 65–85 mm macro feel for the intent lens and ExitTogether details.
- Slow dolly push or controlled lateral slider; avoid FPV and bullet-time presets.
- Keep each generated shot between five and seven seconds.
- Lock the source composition before adding camera motion.
- Use the Seedance motion prompts above as the scene direction.
- Generate landscape and vertical versions separately instead of cropping faces or key objects later.

## Required screen recordings

AI video should provide the hook and transitions. The proof must come from the real product:

1. Search SpaceX and rotate the asset model.
2. Switch between Live, Building, Vision and Risk.
3. Enter “I want to own SpaceX stock” and show the clear explanation.
4. Show the public agent/API response briefly.
5. Show the live ExitTogether batch at `37,250 / 50,000`.
6. Connect the wallet and show the X Layer testnet transaction.
7. End on `https://trueguard-rwa.netlify.app`.

## Accuracy guardrails

- Do not say the user owns SpaceX through SPCX.
- Do not call the demo token a real RWA.
- Do not guarantee a better price or successful settlement.
- Say ExitTogether **seeks** one large-order quote.
- Do not imply an OKX, X Layer, SpaceX, Tesla, Paxos or issuer partnership.
- Keep `Research is not investment advice` in the final end card or X post thread.
