### Project Description

TrueGuard is the AI-RWA understanding and exit layer on X Layer. It shows people and AI agents what a real asset does, what an RWA token actually gives its holder, and which claims are live, being built, or only a vision. Its intent check catches mistakes such as asking to own SpaceX while choosing a price derivative. ExitTogether then lets holders of the exact same RWA token pool small sell orders, keep their own minimum prices, and accept one large-order quote through an X Layer escrow contract. The live demo includes a public API, agent tools, verified sources, and an open X Layer testnet batch.
